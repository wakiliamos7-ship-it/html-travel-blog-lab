Adventures in Fire Island

Trip Summary

My recent trip to Fire Island National Seashore was a perfect escape from the city. I spent my days walking along the sandy beaches, exploring boardwalks through the dunes, and climbing the historic Fire Island Lighthouse for sweeping ocean views.

Highlights included spotting deer in the Sunken Forest, watching the sunset over the Atlantic, and enjoying fresh seafood in the small beach communities. It's a car-free paradise that I can't wait to visit again.
